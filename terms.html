02Who can use BISS

You must be at least 16 years old — or the minimum age required in your country — to use BISS. You must be at least 18 to enter contests or win prizes. Every account belongs to one real, verified person. No bots, no shared logins, no accounts created to impersonate someone else.

03Your account

To keep BISS free of fakes, every account is verified as a real human. You agree to give accurate verification information and to keep your login secure. You're responsible for activity on your account.

FlipSide identities

You post under a permanent pseudonym by default, and can choose to publish under your verified real name story-by-story. Your real name is never shown unless you choose to show it. Your pseudonym is permanent by design — a reputation isn't worth much if you can swap the name the moment it gets spicy.

To run BISS, you grant us a worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free license to host, display, format, translate, and — in aggregated, anonymized form — analyze your content so the platform can work: turning your words into a titled story, generating a score, building Brand Pages, and producing aggregated brand intelligence.

  • This license lasts only as long as your content is on BISS. Delete it and the license ends — we remove it and anonymize it on the back end.
  • You're responsible for what you post. Tell the truth, keep it your own experience, and don't post anything unlawful.
  • We may format, split (one rant mentioning three brands becomes three stories), and lightly redact your content — scrubbing personal data and softening the sharpest edges — but we never change the substance of what you felt.

05Scores and no stars

BISS never uses star ratings. Scores are inferred by AI from the words and emotion in real stories. Scores are an automated read of sentiment, not a statement of fact about any brand, and they move as the stories move.

  • Break the law, infringe others' rights, or interfere with the platform's security.

Full details are in our Community Guidelines, which are part of these Terms.

Contests are run by brands, businesses, and creators on BISS. Entering and winning are governed by our Contest Rules. BISS runs the arena, keeps fakes out, and keeps it fair — we never take a cut of your idea and never judge on a sponsor's behalf.

08Brands and business data

Businesses can subscribe to see aggregated, anonymized intelligence about their own brand — what people feel and why. Businesses never see your identity unless you deliberately publish a story under your real name. We don't sell your stories to advertisers, and we don't run ads.
