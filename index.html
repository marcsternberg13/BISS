Let's be honest about your current tools · because I said so

They sell you the performance. Never the truth.

There's a gap between what customers say in public and what they actually think. That gap is the whole business.

Social listening

$15–40K/yr to show you public posts — only what people say out loud, never what they actually think.

Yelp / reviews

Star ratings anyone can rig, 30%+ fake — zero operational depth a manager can use Monday.

Surveys

~20–30% reply — 70%+ never answer — inside an artificial room that makes people lie. Formality kills honesty.

$15–40K/yr for public posts  →  From $50/mo for what they actually think.

Up to ~98% cheaper at entry — and it's a class of data they structurally cannot collect.
