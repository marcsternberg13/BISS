Why we started

The review economy is broken. We got tired of pretending it wasn't.

Stars are meaningless. Reviews are faked. Surveys vanish into a void. And the one thing worth capturing — how something actually made you feel — had nowhere to go that mattered. Every honest rant died alone in a comment section nobody with power ever read.

So we asked a simple question: what if telling the truth about an experience was as easy as talking to someone who gets it — and what if that truth went straight to the people who could act on it? That question became BISS.

The name

Because I Said So. Your experience doesn't need a citation, a receipt, or five stars to be true. It happened to you. That's all the authority you'll ever need.

Where this is going

Travel is the launch. Life is the destination.

We start with the experiences that hit hardest — flights, hotels, road trips, the meal that broke your heart. But this was never "another review app." Anywhere something's worth having a feeling about, we're coming. It's the place the whole planet comes to tell the truth about everything.

We're not here to sell your data or rate your life out of five. We're here so the truth finally has somewhere to go.
