How it works

Just talk. We write your story for you.

You talk. It writes you into something that sounds smart. Sorcery.

The entire tutorial: you talk, BISS writes. There's no step two. You start, and the second you do, the face reacts — it feels what you feel, in real time. You mention it was your mom's birthday and it leans in and asks about that, because it caught the part that actually hurt.

Then it pulls off the trick. Your furious, half-typed, one-thumbed spiral comes back as a polished, titled, genuinely good story — "The Three-Hour Delta Nightmare" — the kind you'd screenshot and send to the group chat. Mentioned three brands without noticing? It quietly splits your rant into three, one for each.

The twist

Your journal entry is the review. You wrote a story. The world reads the most useful review it's ever seen — no star slider, no "rate 1 to 5," no form. Same act. Wildly better result.

The part you'll screenshot

A face that feels what you feel as you type.

It's staring at you right now. You're going to write it a novel.

Get heated, it gets heated. Soften, it softens. Hit the part where you were genuinely wrecked with joy, and it lights up right alongside you. Not a bouncy little mascot — a mirror. The platform looking you dead in the eye going "yeah. same. keep going."

And you will keep going, because for once something is reacting like a real person would. Basic looks are free forever. The premium ones — the full emotional range, the deluxe Righteous Anger — you unlock. Some stories deserve the good face.

A promise, not a setting

No star ratings. Your story sets the score.

Rating your life out of five is a party trick for people with no feelings.

You will never see a star slider on BISS. Not once. That's a blood oath. Stars are a scam — they mash everything you felt into one lonely number that gets averaged into mush. The AI reads your actual words — the emotion, the specifics, the why — and the score comes from your story, never from a number you got bullied into picking.

Which means you literally can't get it wrong. And nobody can rig it — no fake five-star armies, no brigades. You bring the truth. We do the math.
