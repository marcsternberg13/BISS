What Each Phase Proves

Every phase retires a specific risk and produces the evidence the next stage — and the next investor conversation — depends on. The roadmap is sequenced so that de-risking, not spending, drives the timeline.

Build · M0–M9 — "the product exists"

A working, moderated, web-first platform — therapeutic AI conversation, brand pages, the authenticity system, and the B2B intelligence dashboard — ready for real users and real partners.

Alpha · M10–M16 — "the thesis is real"

Invite-only pilots with strategic partners reach ~200 Member stories per focus brand and surface intelligence a survey never could. The first pilots convert to paid.

Beta · M16–M22 — "the economics work"

National expansion into restaurants and car rentals, documented unit economics (target LTV/CAC 3–6×), and 3–5 confirmed B2B customers — the milestone that opens the Series A conversation.

Launch · M22–M24 — "it scales"

Open signups, native apps, the full community-growth engine, and the deferred product surface (FlipSide and the v2+ roadmap) coming online as the Series A closes.

The launch triangle powers every phase

Each stage runs on the same three legs — an anchor partner, a licensed Sourced corpus, and contest-driven Member stories. Only Member stories become sellable intelligence; Sourced and Member content are always tracked separately, never blended.

On the partners named here

The brands referenced across this roadmap are strategic targets selected for fit — not signed or confirmed relationships. They illustrate the go-to-market logic and the ideal partner profile: customer-obsessed brands with an intelligence gap BISS fills, and no competitive overlap.
